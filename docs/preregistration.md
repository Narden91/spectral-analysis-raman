# Fixed Post Hoc Biological Hypothesis Record

## Primary exploratory hypothesis

H0: The tentatively assigned `carotenoid_1525 / lipid_carbonyl_1721` Raman ratio is not associated with delta MoCA.

H1: The ratio is negatively associated with delta MoCA.

## Analysis framework

- Test: partial Spearman correlation
- Covariates: age and sex
- Inference: exploratory current-cohort analysis with family-adjusted p-values
- Population: disease-only cohort
- Provenance: `post_hoc_fixed`; no independently verifiable pre-analysis registration is available

## Interpretation boundary

This ratio is a tentatively assigned spectral proxy. It has not been validated against an independent biochemical oxidative-stress assay and cannot establish abundance, mechanism, or causation. Independent future registration and external validation are required for confirmatory use.
